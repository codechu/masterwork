# Code of Conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Assume good faith, critique work rather
than people, and keep discussion focused on making the line more honest.

This repository is about characters models speak for themselves, and about
verdicts a thing is not allowed to give itself. The same applies here:
argue against the work, not the person who brought it, and say what you
measured rather than what you assume.

Harassment, personal attacks, and dismissive or demeaning behaviour are not
welcome. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues,
and pull requests that violate this, and may block repeat offenders.

To report a problem, open a confidential issue or contact the maintainers
via the repository's security/contact channels.
